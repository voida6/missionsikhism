Mann's work on the manuscript history of the Sikh scripture is the reason this
site can be specific about the compilation of the Adi Granth rather than vague.
The article is cited here for its account of the scriptural text's expansion,
and for the connection it draws between that and the founding of Sikh towns in
central Punjab in the later sixteenth century.
