Anyone writing about Guru Nanak has to decide how hard to press the
*janam-sakhis*, which are devotional narratives compiled well after the events
they describe. Mann's appraisal sets out what the sources will bear — including
why the birth date is still argued over, and what the 1969 quincentenary settled
in practice rather than in evidence.
