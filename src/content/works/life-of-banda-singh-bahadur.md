Ganda Singh's method is the reason this book still stands. Judgements about
Banda are unusually tangled in the tradition: the Punjabi chronicles are
coloured by a later dispute between the Tat Khalsa and the Bandai Khalsa over
whether Banda had claimed too much for himself, and cannot be read straight.

He therefore worked mainly from Persian sources — Mughal court histories,
newsletters, and records produced by people with every reason to diminish his
subject — and reconstructed the campaign from what they conceded.

It is worth a reader of this site looking at, because it is the same argument
this site makes about sourcing, made ninety years ago and at far greater cost.
