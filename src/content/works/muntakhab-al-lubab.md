Khafi Khan wrote from inside the imperial establishment and had no sympathy
whatever for the Sikhs; the language he uses about them is abusive throughout.
That is precisely why the concessions in his account carry weight. A hostile
court historian recording that imperial forces could not withstand the Sikhs in
several parts of the Punjab, and that a functioning civil administration existed
in the territory they had taken, is not reporting propaganda in his own favour.

Historians of the period use him carefully and against the grain. Ganda Singh's
biography of Banda Singh was built deliberately from Persian sources of this
kind rather than from the Punjabi chronicles.

The linked scan is the Persian original in the 1869 Calcutta edition, not an
English translation.
